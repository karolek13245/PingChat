Features

Everything you need to stay connected

PingChat is purpose-built for fast, private conversations. No bloat, no distractions.

⚡

Instant delivery

Messages arrive in real time over persistent WebSocket connections. No polling, no delays.

🔒

End-to-end encryption

Every message is encrypted before it leaves your device. Not even our servers can read your chats.

@

@username search

Find anyone instantly by their @username. No phone number or email address needed.

👤

Custom profiles

Upload a photo or pick your avatar color. Make PingChat feel like yours.

🌐

Multi-language

English, Polish, Spanish, French, German — speak in the language that feels natural.

◐

Dark & light mode

Switch themes on the fly. The interface adapts to your environment and preference.

Privacy

Your conversations stay between you

PingChat is built around zero-knowledge principles. Messages are encrypted before they leave your device and stored with row-level policies.

🚫

No ads

Your conversations are never used to target you.

🔍

No tracking

No analytics, telemetry, or unnecessary data collection.

💾

No data sales

Your data is never sold, shared, or monetized.

🗑️

Full deletion

Delete your account and its data permanently.
